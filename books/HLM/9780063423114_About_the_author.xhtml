About the Authors

One of American music’s biggest names, KENNY CHESNEY enters the Country Music Hall of Fame this year. Selling out stadiums for two decades, he’s sold 105 million albums and holds Billboard’s record for Most Country No. 1 hits, which define coming of age in America in the twenty-first century.

Creating a space to work hard, play hard, and love hard in the spirit of celebrating life’s best moments, his music brings people together. Heart Life Music is his first book.

HOLLY GLEASON, a past Rock & Roll Hall of Fame/Case Western Reserve Research Fellow, has written for Rolling Stone, Los Angeles Times, The New York Times, and HITS. Named the Los Angeles Press Club’s Entertainment Journalist of the Year and the National Arts and Entertainment Journalism Association’s Independent Journalist of the Year, her books include the Belmont Book Award–winning Woman Walk the Line and Prine on Prine: Interviews and Encounters.
